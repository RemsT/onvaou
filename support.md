# Support — ONvaOU

## Frequently Asked Questions

**The app is loading for a long time on first launch**
ONvaOU downloads official SNCF train schedules on first launch. This can take 1 to 2 minutes depending on your internet connection. This only happens once.

**No results are showing for my search**
- Make sure at least one filter is active (travel time or budget)
- Try widening your time range or increasing your maximum time/budget
- Some smaller stations may have limited connections in the GTFS data

**The prices shown seem incorrect**
Prices displayed in ONvaOU are **estimates only** and do not reflect actual ticket prices. For exact fares and booking, please use SNCF Connect or OUI.sncf.

**The schedules seem outdated**
ONvaOU uses official SNCF GTFS data. If the data is more than 6 months old, a banner will appear in the app inviting you to update. Tap it to download the latest schedules.

**The map is not showing destinations**
Make sure your search returned results. If the list is empty, try adjusting your filters. The map only shows destinations found in the search results.
